Owntrace Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Owntrace is an injection log for Android. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter in it.

In short
  • Everything you enter stays on your phone.
  • Owntrace has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics.
  • The app can't connect to the internet: it doesn't ask Android for internet access at all.
  • The developer never receives any of your information.

What the app stores

The medications you add (name, type, default dose and notes) and the injections you log (date and time, dose, injection site, pain level and notes). This is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps can't read. Owntrace doesn't add its own encryption to this data; it's protected by Android's app sandbox and by your phone's own encryption and screen lock.

App lock

If you turn on "Require unlock", Owntrace asks Android to show your phone's own unlock prompt (fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern). Owntrace never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN; Android only tells the app whether unlocking succeeded. While the lock is on, the app also hides its content in the Recents screen and blocks screenshots.

Backups and exports

  • Exports you make. When you export a backup or a CSV file, you choose where it's saved, such as your phone's storage or a cloud drive. Exported files aren't encrypted, so anyone who can open them can read them. If you save one to a cloud service, that service's privacy policy applies to it.
  • Android cloud backup is turned off for Owntrace.
  • Moving to a new phone. If you use Android's phone-to-phone transfer when setting up a new phone, Android may copy Owntrace's data directly to it. The app lock setting is not copied.

Permissions

Owntrace asks only for permission to use your phone's biometric unlock (fingerprint or face), and only uses it to show the unlock prompt when the app lock is on. It does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

Sharing

Owntrace doesn't collect your information, so there is nothing to sell or share with anyone.

Deleting your data

You can delete injections and medications in the app at any time. Uninstalling Owntrace removes all of its data from your phone. Exported files you saved elsewhere aren't affected; delete those yourself.

Children

Owntrace is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
